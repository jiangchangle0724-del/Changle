The photo reader in the avatar maker (src/account/avatar/match.ts) runs on the phone with
Google's MediaPipe, under the Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0):

  face_landmarker.task        MediaPipe Face Landmarker model
                              https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker
  hair_segmenter.tflite       MediaPipe Image Segmenter, hair segmentation model
                              https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter
  vision_wasm_internal.js     MediaPipe Tasks Vision runtime (@mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.2.0),
  vision_wasm_internal.wasm   copied here at build time from node_modules

The files are unmodified. Photos are read in the browser and never sent anywhere.
