隐私政策

随便（changle.ca/eat） · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 1 日

随便 是 changle.ca 上的一个免费小工具，帮朋友们一起选餐厅，由 Steve Jiang 个人运营。有问题请写信到 jiangchangle0724@gmail.com。

我们收集什么

  • Google 登录：只收到你的 Google 账号编号（下次认出你用）和名字，名字最多留 16 个字。不收邮箱、头像和通讯录。开一桌要登录，加入别人的桌不用。
  • 登录后保存：你的饮食卡（过敏、忌口、清真、素食、预算），最近一次去吃的餐厅（一桌的桌号和餐厅名，或者一个人挑中那家的 Google 编号和名字，以及评没评过；下次问你吃得怎么样，只留最近一次），最近一次一个人挑的候选餐厅（最多 12 家的 Google 编号和资料，用来「换一批」，也用来确认你评的是自己挑中的店；下次挑会替换），当天用了几次（开桌和一个人挑加起来，每个账号每天最多 10 次）。
  • 一个人挑：你回答的心情、口味和预算只用来挑这一次的餐厅（为了「换一批」，会和这次的候选餐厅一起留到你下次挑为止）；饮食卡在你登录时照常存到账号里；点了「就它了」的那家按上一条记下。
  • 每一桌：每个人的昵称、语言、饮食卡和投票，以及这桌找餐厅用的位置（选的区域，或房主手机的定位）。同桌的人只看到「有人对花生过敏」这样的汇总，看不到是谁。一桌在最后一次有人操作 3 天后自动删除。
  • 吃完的评分：计入那家餐厅的统计（好、一般、不好的次数，和最近 20 次的等位时间），不记是谁评的。一个人挑的那家只能评一次，账号里只多记一个「评过了」。
  • 你的手机上：浏览器里存着这部手机的随机编号和密钥（断线后回到原来的座位）、昵称、语言、上次选的位置、评过分的桌号和餐厅编号。登录用的 cookie 只在 changle.ca/eat 下有效，60 天后过期。

还有谁会看到

  • Google 地图：找餐厅时，这桌的位置和范围会发给 Google Places；餐厅照片直接从 Google 的服务器加载。这部分适用 Google 隐私政策。餐厅资料每桌现查，跟这桌一起删除；我们长期保留的只有 Google 的餐厅编号和上面的评分统计。
  • Cloudflare：网站运行在 Cloudflare 上，Cloudflare 会把访问日志保留几天，用来排查问题。
  • 我们不卖数据，不用来做广告，也没有第三方追踪或统计工具。

删除

登录后在首页点「删除我的所有数据」，会删掉你的饮食卡、最近一次去吃的记录和一个人挑的结果，并退出登录。当天的使用次数会留到当天结束，不然删一次就能绕过每天 10 次的上限。其他请求请发邮件。

Privacy

Whatever's Fine (changle.ca/eat) · Updated November 1, 2026

Whatever's Fine is a free tool on changle.ca that helps friends pick a restaurant together. It is run by Steve Jiang. Questions: jiangchangle0724@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: only your Google account ID (to recognize you next time) and your first name, up to 16 characters. Not your email, photo or contacts. Starting a table needs sign-in; joining one doesn't.
  • Saved when signed in: your diet card (allergies, foods you avoid, halal, vegetarian, budget), the last place you went to eat (a table's code and restaurant, or the Google ID and name of your solo pick, and whether you rated it; to ask how it was, and only the latest is kept), your last solo search's candidate places (up to 12 Google IDs and their details, for "show me others" and to check that the place you rate is one you were shown; the next search replaces them), and how many times you used it today (tables and solo picks together, 10 per account per day).
  • Picking alone: your answers (mood, cuisine, budget) only steer that search (they're kept with its candidate places, for "show me others", until your next search); your diet card is saved to your account as usual when you're signed in; the place you choose is kept as above.
  • Each table: everyone's nickname, language, diet card and votes, and the location used to search (the area picked, or the host's phone location). The table only sees summaries like "someone is allergic to peanuts", never who. A table is deleted 3 days after its last activity.
  • Ratings after the meal: added to that restaurant's totals (good, okay and bad counts, and the last 20 wait times), without who rated. A solo pick can be rated once; your account only notes that you rated it.
  • On your phone: the browser keeps this phone's random ID and key (to get your seat back after a disconnect), your nickname, language, last location picked, and which tables and places you rated. The sign-in cookie only works under changle.ca/eat and expires after 60 days.

Who else sees it

  • Google Maps: to find restaurants, the table's location and range are sent to Google Places, and restaurant photos load straight from Google's servers. The Google Privacy Policy applies to that. Restaurant details are fetched for each table and deleted with it; all we keep long term are Google's place IDs and the rating totals above.
  • Cloudflare: the site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps access logs for a few days for troubleshooting.
  • We don't sell data, use it for ads, or run third-party tracking or analytics.

Deleting

Signed in, tap "Delete all my data" on the home page. It erases your diet card, your last table or pick and your last solo search, and signs you out. Today's count stays until the day is over; otherwise deleting would get around the limit of 10 a day. For anything else, email us.
