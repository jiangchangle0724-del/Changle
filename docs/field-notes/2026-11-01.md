# 一起跳 A1: timing, 2026-11-01

A1's acceptance (`docs/dance-along.md`): phones' clocks within 30 ms of the speaker's. Three things
to measure: the clock error, the TV's delay that calibration finds (`audioOffsetMs`), and whether
the phones' flashes land on the beat the room hears.

## 1. On one machine (done)

`games/party-server/scripts/timing-check.cjs`: the Worker running locally (`npm run dev`), a
speaker page and three phones (iPhone 13 profile) in Chromium. On one machine the true clock
difference is known exactly (the pages' `performance.timeOrigin`), so the error is measured, not
estimated. Round trips are 1 to 9 ms here, much shorter than over Wi-Fi.

| What | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Clock error, 3 phones | +0.85, +0.9, +0.65 ms (round trips 2.3, 1.2, 1.5 ms) |
| Calibration, taps sent 180 ms after each click | `audioOffsetMs` 181 (the host phone's clock was 0.85 ms off) |
| Flashes against the heard beat | mean +2.0 to +2.8 ms, worst 6.3 ms (24 beats each) |
| A phone reloaded mid-dance | back in its seat, its first flash on the next beat, within 2.6 ms |
| 我在 | the name lit up on the speaker page |

Earlier runs on the same machine, with four other test browsers running, had clock errors up to
15 ms and single flashes 35 to 45 ms late (dropped frames). Two fixes came out of them: a phone now
counts its clock as set after 6 pings, not 1 (the first pings go out while the page is still
loading, and a slow one is off by half its delay: one was 200 ms off), and a phone that comes in
mid-beat waits for the next beat instead of flashing straight away.

## 2. On the real devices (to do, with Steve)

The Worker is online at changle.ca/party since 1 November (`games/party-server/README.md`).

Setup: the Mac opens `changle.ca/party/screen?timing` in Safari (the clock readout; since 12 Nov the
speaker page has no buttons), mirrors to the Apple TV (Control Centre > Screen Mirroring), volume
up, and clicks the page once (and the video it shows, for Safari) to turn its sound on. Each iPhone
scans the QR code in Safari and joins; the first one in is the host.

1. **Clock error.** The Mac shows its clock (`?timing`); on one iPhone, tap 测量. Hold the iPhone next to the Mac's screen
   and take 5 photos with another phone: the difference between the two big numbers is the error
   (plus up to one frame, 17 ms, from the screens refreshing at different moments). Take the median.
   Then the same next to the TV: that difference is the mirroring's picture delay.
2. **Calibration.** The host taps 校准电视延迟 and taps the big button each time the TV clicks. The
   Mac shows the result ("电视比这台设备晚 N 毫秒"). Do it 3 times; note the 3 numbers.
3. **Flashes on the beat.** Host: 开始：120 拍节拍器, then 开始跳. Listen to the TV and watch the
   phones: do the flashes land on the clicks? For a number: film a phone screen next to the TV with
   another iPhone in slow motion (240 fps); in the video, count the frames between the flash and
   the click's sound (1 frame = 4 ms).
